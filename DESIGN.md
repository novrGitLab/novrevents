# NovrEvents design direction

## The opening spread
A photograph shot from inside a crowd gives discovery the intimacy of a festival poster. Plum shadows and a warm paper surface connect it to the violet Cybernovr mark. The public experience is expressive. Organizer and admin screens prioritize legible fields and fast repeat actions.

## Reading order
The landing opens with a way into discovery, followed by upcoming events drawn from the directory, ways to explore by interest, then hosting and planning. An event detail page leads with the event identity, date and place, and keeps registration and its availability visible. The workspace separates attendance, organizing and account management.

## Type and color
DM Sans carries controls and information. Instrument Serif italic marks the editorial line on the poster and provides contrast in section headings. Display type scales with the viewport. Event descriptions use a 65-character reading measure. Use violet #632D8A for actions, cobalt #4353A3 for focus and links, and red #E3192A for brand accents. Error messaging has its own darker text token. Use a warm paper canvas, white controls, and dark ink.

## Composition
Use up to 1280px for inner content and edge-to-edge campaign photography. A wide image uses shadow on the left for the heading. On phones, crop toward the subject and darken the lower image for copy. List rows, chapter rules and restrained rounded controls hold the rest of the site together. Forms retain visible labels, field errors and keyboard focus. Rounded local Phosphor vectors use currentColor. The photo illustrates the brand and must never imply that a listed event took place.

## References
