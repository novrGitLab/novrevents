# NovrEvents design direction

## The opening spread
A lilac poster pairs an oversized ink headline with a stack of photographic ticket covers. A live band, a family reception, a workshop and a conference each lead into a different part of the platform. The same ticket mark and two-part wordmark identify the header, footer, dashboard and authentication pages. The public experience is expressive. Organizer and admin screens prioritize legible fields and fast repeat actions.

## Reading order
The landing opens with a way into discovery, followed by upcoming events drawn from the directory, ways to explore by interest, then hosting and planning. An event detail page leads with the event identity, date and place, and keeps registration and its availability visible. The workspace separates attendance, organizing and account management.

## Type and color
DM Sans carries controls and information. Instrument Serif italic marks the editorial line on the poster and provides contrast in section headings. Display type scales with the viewport. Event descriptions use a 65-character reading measure. Use violet #632D8A for actions, cobalt #4353A3 for focus and links, and red #E3192A for brand accents. Error messaging has its own darker text token. Use a warm paper canvas, white controls, and dark ink.

## Composition
Use up to 1280px for inner content and edge-to-edge campaign photography. Set type and image on separate areas of the paper. On phones the headline leads into a compact ticket deck, then a two-field event finder. List rows, chapter rules and restrained rounded controls hold the rest of the site together. Forms retain visible labels, field errors and keyboard focus. Rounded local Phosphor vectors use currentColor. The photo illustrates the brand and must never imply that a listed event took place.

## Admission ticket identity

The account entry pairs a quiet form with a plum admission poster. A physical cream ticket puppet, with a perforated plum stub and cobalt folded-paper limbs, greets guests there and recurs in the workspace and footer. The original image was generated for NovrEvents and exported as three transparent WebP sizes. On a phone the pocket-size character takes the place of the poster. Workspace fields sit on a cool gray canvas. Standard buttons and inputs share a 48 px height and a 12 px corner radius. Single-color icons use the surrounding text color.
